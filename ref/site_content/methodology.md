# Methodology

Healthcare Intel Digest is generated from a consistent company universe and a reproducible
weekly reporting process. Each report records its market-data date, source outcomes, and the
inputs needed to rebuild the published edition.

## Company universe

The watchlist is organized into healthcare subcategories such as payers, provider technology,
care delivery, revenue cycle, and related services. A company may appear in more than one
subcategory when its business spans those markets. The current universe is maintained in the
project's company configuration.

## Market performance

- Price returns are calculated over trailing 3-, 12-, and 24-month windows using saved daily
  closing prices.
- Charts index each series to 100 at the beginning of the displayed window so differently
  priced securities can be compared directly.
- SPY is used as the broad-market benchmark.
- Subcategory returns are weighted using the most recently available company market
  capitalizations. Missing observations are excluded rather than imputed.
- Rankings are calculated across the full watchlist and within each subcategory. Changes are
  compared with the most recent eligible prior final report.

## Earnings coverage

Public earnings pages are checked for report dates, transcript summaries, at-a-glance items,
and key moments. A company appears in Recent Earnings Highlights when its saved report date
falls within the configured weekly window and usable call material is available. Upcoming
dates may be marked tentative when a public source has not yet confirmed them.

Earnings charts compare the featured company with other tracked companies in its subcategory
and with SPY over the preceding three months. These charts show price movement, not business
fundamentals or total shareholder return.

## Strategy narrative

The weekly narrative synthesizes publicly available company, regulatory, and market
developments. Links are retained where available so readers can inspect source material. The
narrative is analysis rather than a complete record of every event in the sector.

## Timing and limitations

Market prices, market capitalizations, earnings dates, and public web pages update on different
schedules. A report may therefore contain clearly identified warnings or stale secondary data
while retaining valid market analysis. Corporate actions, symbol changes, revisions, and gaps
in source availability can also affect historical comparisons.

The report is informational and is not investment advice. Readers should verify material facts
against primary filings, company disclosures, and regulatory publications.
