What this is

Evocative, not a facsimile

Chief Ethanol’s site is a Divi plant homepage: a red badge, Factoria headlines in #D22630, uppercase nav, square red buttons, and full-bleed photography of steel and grain. This theme keeps that register — Midwest industrial, one hot accent, slab type, tracked labels — and changes the fingerprints.

Color

Barn red, not badge red

Warmer and a step darker than Chief’s mark. Headings stay ink; red is reserved for kickers, links, and fills.

Type

Zilla Slab + Source Sans 3

A stout slab and a technical sans. Same job as Factoria and Vista Sans; different cut, freely licensed.

Surface

Grain-dust paper

Off-white instead of pure white, with a harvest-gold hairline. Feels like a mill office, not a cloned homepage.

Why most “ethanol stocks” are not ethanol companies

August 20, 2026 · Companies · 6 min

The listed pure plays that clear a billion dollars of equity value are Green Plains and REX American. Everyone else on the roster is a grain merchant, a refiner, a fertilizer company, or a railroad that happens to live on this chain.

POET still sets the operating benchmark, and POET does not trade.

That is the point of covering the value chain instead of a ticker screen. Corn acres set feedstock. Crush margins set plant cash. RINs and 45Z set the policy overlay. Railcars set whether a gallon in Iowa is a gallon in Newark.

What belongs in the first posts

A crush-margin explainer, a plant map, and the company roster already in companies.md. The stylesheet is meant to carry those pages without looking like a cloned mill site.
